Round X1 · How the sky changes

Until now a new state replaced the sky at once. The sky changes in two ways: data syncs (a new readiness score, last night's sleep, signs of illness) and the hour moves. The hour now follows the clock once a minute, eased, so it never jumps. For a data sync, there are three ways to bring in the new sky. They work the same live and with baked frames. The old picture holds until the new one is fully drawn, so no half-drawn frame ever shows. Then it gives way:

A · Fade, 1.8 s: the whole sky dissolves into the new one. B · Front, 3.5 s: the new weather blows in from the left behind a soft, cloud-torn edge. C · Patches, 3 s: the new sky opens in soft cloud-shaped patches. With reduced motion turned on, every style becomes a plain 1.2 s fade.

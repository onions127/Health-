Round B1 · The weather of the body, and the mountain range

Each body signal owns one layer of the sky (PLAN-sky D1), so the sky never says something the numbers do not. All thresholds are against your own normal. The mapping is one pure function, BodySky.bodySky(body, hour) in body-sky.js: SkyState out, plus the note you get when you tap the sky (D4) and the same text for TalkBack.

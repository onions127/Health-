Round 3.6 · Stratus, stratocumulus, fog and light rain

New sky types on the same scene. Low sheet clouds run from stratocumulus (lumpy cells with gaps of blue) to stratus (an unbroken grey ceiling). Fog pools in the hollows between the hills and drifts in banks. Rain falls in three depths of streaks and veils the distance. Captions say which body state each sky stands for (agreed mapping, PLAN-sky.md).

Painted sky, after the weather app

A second cloud style: a flat, soft layer seen almost straight up. Airbrushed white masses with pale-blue shade on the side away from the light, a misty halo at the edges, saturated blue gaps and a fine grain. It is built from the same billow noise as the 3D clouds, so it stays code-drawn and can still follow the body data (cover shown at three levels). It matches the reference's style; it is not a copy of its artwork.

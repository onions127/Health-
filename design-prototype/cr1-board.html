Round CR1 · The rules that pick Coach's read

Coach's headline over the sky, and the line of numbers under it, were picked by hand for each day we drew. coach-rules.js now picks them: one function of the body and the hour, like BodySky, using BodySky's own thresholds for haze, likely illness, the storm and pace, so the sky and the read cannot disagree. Per part of the day, the first rule that matches wins. The detail line always holds the numbers the read rests on.

The answers in the chat stay as drawn: they show the kind of answer Coach gives and are not picked by rules.

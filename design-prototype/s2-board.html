Round S2 · Sleep: hours in the night, and other nights

A · Last night (picked in S1), with two changes. The time: the stages chart has a faint line at each full hour and the hour under it (23, 00, 01 … 06), so you can see when each stage happened without the lines taking over. Other nights: the back and forward buttons at the top right step through the last 30 nights. Forward is dimmed on last night. Every number, the stages and the times follow the night shown. The phone below works: tap the buttons.

Only last night has real stage data in the prototype. The older nights are made from it, scaled to each night's real sleep time with a varied bedtime, so the layout can be judged.

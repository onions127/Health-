Round BD5 · The sample data from the app's formulas, and a stricter sleep score

The prototype's readiness and sleep scores were set by hand. scores.js now holds the app's formulas, ported from android-app (sleep score, readiness, recovery, daily score, "your normal"), and data.js scores all 90 days with them, each day against the days before it.

That showed a problem: the app's sleep score barely drops after a poor night. Its duration part is minutes ÷ 7h30 (4h40 still earns 62 of 100), and efficiency is the raw percentage (a broken 78% night earns 78). Over the 90 days it reads 82–99, and a 4h40 night that starts at 01:20 scores 73. The sky's morning fog (from 78 down) almost never shows, and the bad days we designed can't happen. S2 keeps the five parts and their weights and changes three ranges so a poor night costs points. The boards run S2; add ?sleep=app to any board to see the app's.

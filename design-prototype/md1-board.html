Round MD1 · A metric's page, and the Calendar

Tapping a row in Trends opens that metric's page. Shipped, it is graphite: back arrow and name; a hero card (today's value, a "Normal 55–63" chip, an insight chip); Week / Month / 3M / Year; a chart card (the picked day's value and chip, a smoothed line, daily dots, days outside normal marked; tap to pick a day); how the score is made (score metrics only); and the 30-day average. The Calendar (Trends' top-right button) is a 14-day grid with a coloured dot per day; tapping a day opens its Today.

Drawn here for HRV on today's sky (15:10), G2 day glass, TR2's good/bad colours (HRV above normal = good, green; below = coral, always with the arrow and word). Prototype data: the last 30 days of data.js, today 64 ms, normal 55–63 ms.

Round T4 · Mountain range, tree foliage

Your T3 picks: B (open and calm) and the maple. Asked: make the mountain more of a range, not the whole horizon; the tree textures are the weakest part ("they look really bad").

Mountain: now a range over about half the horizon (left of centre to just past it), five or six summits of different heights with sharp ridged crests, ending in open sky on both sides. Its detail lives only on the massif, so the rest of the horizon stays the low hills.

Trees, rebuilt: the crown is lit as one big form in a few painted tones (light, mid, shade, underside), the way illustrators paint foliage. The leaf texture lives only in the edges: clump shapes break the silhouette and the borders between the tones, at two scales (masses and leaf clumps). Autumn thinning opens rounded gaps from the rim inward; sun shining through the brightest edge; a few leaf glints. Limbs fan upward with side branches; bare winter keeps the soft twig haze.

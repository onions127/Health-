Round TR2 · Trends: the list, with good and bad

A · List (picked in TR1). When a metric strays from your normal, its word, arrow and today's dot take a colour: green when the change is good for you (higher HRV, lower resting HR, more sleep, ahead of your step pace) and coral when it is not (the opposite, a raised skin temperature, low blood oxygen). Changes that are neither (strain above or below your usual) stay in the ink. Colour is never alone: the word ("Above normal") and the arrow say the same thing, so it reads in grey too. A thin bar at the row's left edge in the same colour lets you scan the list.

Two days: today at 15:10 (day glass; three changes, all good) and 4 Nov, a poor day, at 23:10 (night glass; most changes bad). Each glass has its own pair of colours, measured to 3:1 against the card.

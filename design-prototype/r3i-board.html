Technique 5 · The sky is baked, the motion stays live

Games pre-render what is expensive and add what moves on top. Here the PC renders the sky and land once per keyframe (6 hours of a fair day, one cloudier noon) with the full shader and saves the four depth layers as images. The phone only loads them and runs the cheap passes: tilt, wind, birds, the sun disc where the hour puts it, bloom and the grade. Between keyframes the layers blend in linear light; the near clouds keep drifting by sliding their layer into extra room baked beside it. Left renders live, right plays the bake.

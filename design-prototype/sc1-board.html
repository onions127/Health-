Round SC1 · Scrolling over the sky

Before SC1, a page that scrolls (Sleep, S4) took the whole picture with it: sky, land and all slid away 1:1. SC1 follows the reference (Samsung Weather, measured on the S21 from a slow-scroll video and its view tree): the sky, the sun and the page under the cards stay put; over the first 200 px of scroll the near land lifts a little (.15 of the scroll, 30 px at most), blurs and fades away, the far hills and the lake blur with it; the header stays pinned and the page clips under it; the hero's word and detail line fade over the first ~90 px while the number stays and scrolls off with the page. Scrolling back plays it in reverse: it follows the finger, not a timer.

Shown on Sleep, the one page long enough to scroll.

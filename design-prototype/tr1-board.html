Round TR1 · Trends

Trends is the third tab. Shipped, it is a graphite list: one row per metric with a 30-day sparkline, today's value turned red when it is outside your normal, and a Calendar button. Here it moves onto the scene and glass. It shows the same sky as Today at this hour (15:10) and takes the place of Today's number with a count: how many of your numbers are within your normal. Sleep keeps its row, and the row opens the Sleep tab, so its charts are not drawn twice. Steps are judged against your usual pace for this time of day, not a full day (brief rule).

Outside normal is never shown by colour alone: the row says "Above normal" or "Below normal", and the day's dot is ringed. Every metric opens its detail (range, chart, difference from your average), unchanged for now.

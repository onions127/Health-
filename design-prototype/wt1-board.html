Round WT1 · Log weight

Logging weight is one tap from Today (the brief), from the top-right button. Shipped, it is a graphite bottom sheet: a decimal field prefilled with your last weight and selected, the unit, a Date button (any day in the loaded series), "Replaces 64.2 kg" and Update when that day already has an entry, a range check (30–300 kg), and a "Saved … · Undo" snackbar. Weight feeds the calorie estimate and the Trends weight row.

Here it is a glass sheet over Today, like the morning check (MC1 B), at 07:15 just after the check. Two ways to enter the number; both keep every shipped rule (prefill, date, Replaces/Update, range, Undo). Prototype data: last entry 64.2 kg on 17 Nov.

Round S3 · Sleep: trends below the fold

The shipped Sleep Trends screen (duration, bedtime → wake, score, stage mix by week) moves below the fold of the Sleep tab, in the same glass and ink. The scene scrolls away and the page keeps its night colour under the cards. One rule ties the screen together: the night you are looking at is the bright one in every chart. Tap any night in a chart to open it above; the back/forward buttons move the highlight too.

Late nights were red in the shipped app. Here they are hollow (an outline, not a fill), so the shape carries the meaning, not a colour. Every mark is the ink at a measured strength over the glass on the page colour (no veil, floor 3.0).
